This journal is where we publish what we're learning as we work: notes on
Total Experience Design, observations from the projects and places we
visit, research we're following, and news from the studio.

Adaptive and multi-use spaces are changing quickly — in how they're
funded, programmed, operated and experienced. Much of what we write here
will sit in that gap between strategic ambition and operational reality,
because that's where our work lives too.

Expect entries across six threads:

- **News** — what's happening at Interval.
- **Articles** — longer-form thinking on Total Experience Design.
- **Interests** — places, projects and ideas we're learning from.
- **Event reports** — notes from the rooms we're in.
- **Research** — studies and benchmarks we're building or following.
- **Projects** — selected work, when we can share it.

If a piece here speaks to something you're building, write to us at
[hello@intervaladvisory.co.uk](mailto:hello@intervaladvisory.co.uk).
